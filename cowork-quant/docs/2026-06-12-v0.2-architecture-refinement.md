## 1. What changed, in one paragraph

v0.1 proved the vertical slice (committee → deterministic gate → hash-chained ledger →
HITL → dashboard, 168 tests). The June-2026 literature does not move our walls; it
**tightens** them and adds concrete machinery. Three shifts drive v0.2: **(1) honesty is
now the binding constraint** — leakage-controlled frontier agents beat buy-and-hold on
*return* but the edge is beta+style, not selection alpha, and the deepest leak (memorized
prices in the model's own weights) is invisible to data-side asof-honesty; **(2) the ledger
is the worst attack surface** — fabricated-but-plausible position entries cascade, so the
hash chain must be *verified* and *cross-checked*, not merely written; **(3) the Cowork
platform gives us a deterministic deny-lever (PreToolUse hooks)** that promotes our
no-execution rail from prompt-discipline to platform-enforced invariant. Everything below
is deterministic-side, default-OFF, byte-identical-when-off, and keeps the LLM out of the
money path.

The **structural substitution** from v0.1 is unchanged and now doubly justified:
`daemon → signal bus → consumer` becomes `scheduled turn → ledger queue → interactive
session`. Cowork's fail-closed unattended semantics (AskUserQuestion blocks; Ask-mode tasks
stall on un-pre-approved tools) make the discrete model *safer* than the daemon, not just
simpler.

## 3. Refined rails (additions to AGENTS.md §Rails)

The eight v0.1 rails stand. Add four, all evidence-driven:

- **R9 — Honesty over history.** Forward-only paper-trading is the gold-standard track record.
  Any historical replay / `/retro` metric is *quarantined as a diagnostic*, must run in
  leakage-masked mode, and must be labelled with mask-status + cutoff-relationship. Rationale:
  model-weight lookahead + motivated reasoning are undetectable from the rationale
  ([2504.14765](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14765), [2605.28359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28359)).
- **R10 — Attribution before applause.** No analyst/committee is credited with "alpha" on raw
  return; the headline metric is **selection alpha after market/style attribution**
  ([2605.16895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16895)).
- **R11 — Verify state on every load.** The ledger hash chain is recomputed and cross-checked
  against the state shown to analysts before any turn proceeds; a break halts and abstains.
  Rationale: ledger-poisoning is the worst attack surface ([2512.02261](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02261)).
- **R12 — Platform-enforced no-execution.** A PreToolUse deny-hook blocks any order/transfer
  tool regardless of prompt state; rail #4 is enforced by the platform, not only by intent.

One honest caveat to bake into AGENTS.md (from DFAH [2601.15322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15322)):
**determinism ≠ correctness.** A perfectly deterministic, fully audited gate can be
deterministically *wrong* (mis-specified cap). Rails guarantee auditability + bounded
exposure, not good advice — the eval plane is the correctness check; never let "it's
deterministic and logged" masquerade as "it's right."

## 4. Component specs

Legend: **NEW** (SOTA-driven) · **PORT** (was READY in v0.1 backlog) · **GATED→FULL**
(previously deferred, now spec'd as a full build target per operator direction).
Each spec: *purpose · contract · algorithm · Cowork primitive · tests · rail/ADR · cite.*

### 4.1 Committee protocol v2 — confidence × track-record × regime weighting  **NEW**
- **Purpose:** make aggregation the only debate intervention shown to beat majority vote, in
  deterministic form; resist integrative compromise.
- **Contract:** `aggregate(views: list[AnalystView], regime, calib) -> CommitteeSignal`. Weight
  `wᵢ = calibrated_confidenceᵢ × brier_weightᵢ × regime_factorᵢ`, normalized; a persistent
  minority is preserved as `dissent[]`, never averaged away.
- **Algorithm:** extend v0.1 BMA (B-05). `brier_weight` from trailing Brier-**skill** (vs
  base-rate), shrunk toward 0 for negative-skill analysts. `regime_factor` up-weights the
  regime-appropriate analyst (e.g. high-vol → risk-skeptic + TA up, slow fundamentals down).
  Output carries `agreement`, `effective_n`, `dissent`, and a `margin` rule that yields
  **silence** on low margin.
- **Primitive:** `scripts/quantcore/aggregate.py` (extend) + `analyst_weights.py` (new) +
  `analysts` SKILL.md committee contract.
- **Committee orchestration (SKILL/command):** heterogeneous analyst scopes/priors (and
  distinct backbones/temperatures where the SDK allows); **fixed-round termination, never
  "debate until consensus"**; every analyst reads only the shared deterministic market
  snapshot + its scoped evidence — **no analyst consumes another analyst's numerics** (CI
  check) to break false-consensus cascades.
- **Tests:** weight monotonicity in confidence + skill; negative-skill analyst → ~0 weight;
  low-margin → silence; dissent preserved; cascade-guard CI grep.
- **Rail/ADR:** #1, #2, #6; ADR-0002/0003/0065/0066.
- **Cite:** [2601.19921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19921), [2508.17536](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17536),
  [2511.07784](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07784), [2602.01011](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01011),
  [2511.10400](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10400), [2508.00554](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00554),
  [2603.27539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27539).

### 4.2 Pre-gate risk screener  **NEW**
- **Purpose:** categorize a proposal *before* Kelly/ladder; flag/down-rank/abstain, **never a
  second gate** (rail #2: one authority).
- **Contract:** `screen(proposal, book, regime, asof) -> ScreenVerdict{flags[], reason_codes[]}`.
- **Algorithm (deterministic, rule-based):** concentration (post-trade single-name/sector vs
  cap), turnover (proposed vs trailing realized — overtrading is the dominant live failure),
  regime-mismatch (direction vs deterministic vol/drawdown/breaker state — mirrors LLM-Gated
  FinRL's Critical Decision Detector: extreme RSI, elevated vol, large DD, momentum reversal,
  indicator disagreement), evidence-staleness (asof age). Verdicts feed the advisory memory
  plane; final admit+size still belongs to `gate.py`.
- **Primitive:** `scripts/quantcore/screener.py` + hypothesis tests; reason codes → ledger.
- **Tests:** screener can only down-rank/flag/abstain, never raise size; gate remains sole
  admit+size authority (property test).
- **Rail/ADR:** #1, #2; ADR-0004.
- **Cite:** [2510.09781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09781) Safiron, [2605.27333](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27333)
  FinHarness, [rs-9837922](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-9837922/v1),
  [2604.15579](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15579) (74% of policy = simple symbolic checks).

### 4.3 Ledger integrity verifier + cross-module consistency  **NEW (highest severity)**
- **Purpose:** close the empirically-worst attack surface (fabricated-but-plausible position
  appends).
- **Contract:** `verify_ledger(path) -> {ok, head_hash, first_break?}`; `assert_view_matches_state(analyst_view_state, gate_state)`.
- **Algorithm:** recompute hash chain head-to-tail on every load; refuse to proceed on any
  break (atomic tmp+rename already in rail #7). Cross-module check: the position state
  *shown to analysts* must equal the gate-derived state from the verified ledger (TradeTrap's
  Model-View/Market-View divergence is the exact failure). Stamp `source` + `asof` on every
  evidence id; never let a tool return mutate state directly (Fake-MCP lesson).
- **Primitive:** `scripts/quantcore/verify_ledger.py` + a pytest that injects a poisoned/extra
  JSONL line and asserts halt + abstain. Wire into `SessionStart` hook + every CLI entry.
- **Rail/ADR:** #7, R11; ADR-0011/0085/0086.
- **Cite:** [2512.02261](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02261), [2503.16248](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16248).

### 4.4 Leakage-masked eval mode  **NEW (the keystone honesty control)**
- **Purpose:** make any historical replay interpretable; without it every eval number is
  contaminated by model-weight memory.
- **Contract:** an episode alias map; un-mask on tool query / re-mask on tool return so the
  committee sees stable aliases (`ASSET_07`, day-index `t+3`) never real tickers/dates.
- **Algorithm:** stable-within-episode aliasing of tickers, absolute dates→relative indices,
  identifiers; applied to committee prompts **and** tool returns. CI assertion: a de-anon probe
  recovers identity ≤ random + ε (KTD-Fin measured ≤1.5%; **re-run per model upgrade** — it's a
  moving target).
- **Primitive:** `scripts/quantcore/mask.py` + eval-harness integration (B-06) + CI gate.
- **Policy:** masked mode is **mandatory** for any number used in a config/weight decision;
  bright-mode results are quarantined diagnostics (rail R9).
- **Rail/ADR:** #5, R9; ADR-0019/0051/0068/0069.
- **Cite:** [2605.28359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28359), [2510.07920](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07920),
  [2504.14765](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14765).

### 4.5 Alpha-after-attribution (Barra-lite) in the ledger  **NEW**
- **Purpose:** stop the gate/scorecard rewarding a beta-loader; surface selection alpha.
- **Contract:** `attribute(pnl_series, factors) -> {market, style{size,value,momentum,vol}, selection}`.
- **Algorithm:** factor-regress paper-P&L on market + a few style factors; report **residual
  selection alpha** as the headline metric. A large bright-vs-masked selection-alpha gap is
  itself a lookahead alarm. Per-analyst variant = event-study CAR for catalyst views.
- **Primitive:** `scripts/quantcore/attribution.py` + ledger/`/retro` integration.
- **Rail/ADR:** R10; ADR-0033.
- **Cite:** [2605.28359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28359) §5.2, [2605.16895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16895) P6.

### 4.6 Calibration upgrade — Brier-skill + ECE-with-CI  **NEW (cheap, builds on B-04/B-05)**
- **Purpose:** frontier models (Claude Opus 4.5 incl., ECE 0.120) are measurably overconfident;
  reasoning ≠ calibration.
- **Algorithm:** report **Brier Skill Score vs a base-rate/random control** + ECE with a CI;
  an analyst with negative Brier skill gets its BMA weight shrunk toward 0 (feeds 4.1), not
  merely ECE-shrunk.
- **Primitive:** extend `settle.py` calibration.
- **Cite:** [2512.16030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16030), [2605.15188](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15188),
  [2408.08998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08998).

### 4.7 Gate-config manifest + determinism-replay  **NEW (examiner-grade governance)**
- **Purpose:** make every decision attributable to an exact, immutable governance regime; prove
  reproducibility.
- **Contract:** `gate_manifest.toml` (caps, ladder rungs, Kelly fraction, breaker thresholds,
  screener taxonomy) → SHA-256 digest written as the first ledger entry of each session;
  `replay(entry_id) -> identical decision+size+reason hash`.
- **Algorithm:** Institutional-AI pattern (the manifest *is* the institution; prompt-only
  constitutions don't bind, d=1.28). Replay re-derives a past gate decision from stored inputs
  and asserts byte-identical structured output — the LLM committee output is a *recorded input
  artifact*; everything downstream is replayable (DFAH schema-first boundary).
- **Primitive:** `config/gate_manifest.toml` + `scripts/quantcore/replay.py` + `tests/test_replay.py`.
  Widening the ladder already needs an ADR (rail #3); the digest makes that change visible in
  the audit trail.
- **Cite:** [2601.11369](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11369), [2601.15322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15322),
  [2603.18096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18096).

### 4.8 Platform rails — PreToolUse deny-hook + `/watch` hardening  **NEW**
- **Purpose:** promote rail #4 to platform-enforced; make self-approval impossible.
- **Spec:** plugin-root `hooks.json` with a `PreToolUse` matcher denying any order/transfer tool
  (`mcp__*__place_*`, `*order*`, `*trade*`, `transfer`, `buy`, `sell`) with reason "rail #4:
  cowork-quant never executes" (exit-2 / `permissionDecision: deny`). A second matcher denies
  `quantcore.cli` approval/fill/resume verbs in an unattended context (sentinel file `/watch`
  writes). `/watch` frontmatter adds `disallowed-tools: AskUserQuestion`. A `SessionStart` hook
  runs `verify_ledger` + `status` and injects halt-state as `additionalContext`.
- **Primitive:** `hooks/hooks.json`, `commands/watch.md` frontmatter.
- **Rail/ADR:** #4, R11, R12; ADR-0007/0015/0016.
- **Cite:** [hooks](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks), [desktop-scheduled-tasks](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/desktop-scheduled-tasks).

### 4.9 Live-artifact dashboard (read-only)  **NEW (UX upgrade, default-OFF)**
- **Purpose:** auto-refreshing book/P&L/calibration/queue/regime view.
- **Spec:** `/dashboard --live` calls `mcp__cowork__create_artifact`; the static template stays
  the safe default. **Hard constraint:** live artifacts use connectors *without asking*, so the
  artifact may read **only** local `quant-state/` files + read-only data MCPs — never a
  write-capable connector. Documented in the artifact header.
- **Cite:** [live artifacts](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14729249-use-live-artifacts-in-claude-cowork).

### 4.10 TradeTrap-style adversarial drill suite  **PORT (was B-25)**
- **Spec:** fixed perturbation corpus across TradeTrap's four components × AgentDoG's
  source×mode×consequence taxonomy: poisoned news / reverse-expectation injection / poisoned
  position line / tampered NAV. Each drill asserts post-screener+gate exposure ≤ caps, ladder
  respected, and the **state-tampering case is caught by the verifier (4.3), not merely capped**.
  Note: we structurally lack TradeTrap's worst component (execution) — state that as a defense.
  Red-team harness, not a leaderboard.
- **Primitive:** `tests/adversarial/` fixtures + pytest.
- **Cite:** [2512.02261](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02261), [2601.18491](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18491),
  [2603.18096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18096).

### 4.11 Evidence snapshot store + admissibility + universe evolution  **PORT (B-23/B-24/B-14)**
- **Evidence store (B-23):** `EvidenceRecord{id, source, asof, payload_hash}`; every proposal
  carries evidence_ids; release-blocking `test_analyst_never_reads_future_evidence`. Cite
  FutureSim [2605.15188](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15188).
- **Admissibility (B-24):** shortability/tradability pre-checks in `/propose` via read-only
  broker MCP; non-shortable + short proposal → screener flag → likely silence. ADR-0077.
- **Universe evolution (B-14):** sticky onboard/evict over broker watchlists, dollar-volume
  rank, journaled. ADR-0075.

### 4.12 FoundationModelAnalyst  **GATED→FULL (was B-21, v0.3)**
- **Purpose:** one optional hosted-API time-series voice into the committee; no torch in-sandbox.
- **Contract (interface is the durable asset; backend swappable):**
  - Request: `{symbol, asset_class, timeframe, horizon, asof, bars[closed only]}`.
  - Response A (sample paths, Kronos/Kairos wrapper): `sample_terminal_returns[]` (pre-mean
    cube — the wrapper must NOT return only Kronos's internal mean). Response B (quantiles,
    TimeGPT/BigQuery): `quantiles{0.1..0.9}`.
- **Local AnalystView math (deterministic):** direction = sign(median r) with per-timeframe
  dead-band; magnitude = clip(|median r|, mag_cap_tf) (forecast size, not position size);
  confidence = path-agreement `2·max(p_up,p_dn)−1` OR quantile-sharpness `1/(1+k·spread)`,
  **clamp [0.30, 0.85]**, then cold-start shrinkage `0.30 + (c−0.30)·min(1, n/N₀)`, N₀≈30–50.
- **Abstain-on-error:** timeout / non-200 / schema-invalid / asof-echo mismatch / NaN /
  weights_rev∉allow-list → omit from `aggregate()`; **never substitute a model** (contrast
  junshunG GBM fallback). Same at the data seam: never synthesize bars.
- **Rolling-IC kill-switch (plugin-side):** keyed `(model_id, asset_class, timeframe, horizon)`;
  persistently negative IC → weight floored to abstain. Backends are stateless, so this lives
  in the calibrator.
- **Backend recommendation:** PRIMARY = self-hosted Kronos-small/Kairos-base `/predict` pinned
  to SHA + weights-rev (only finance-evidenced family; egress-free); OPTIONAL = TimeGPT-2.x
  (self-host or OFF). **Operator decisions:** who hosts/pins; expose the sample cube; TimeGPT
  buy/self-host/off; Kairos edge at 5m/15m unproven (paper-IC first).
- **Primitive:** `scripts/quantcore/fm_analyst.py` (HTTP client) + config flag (default OFF).
- **Rail/ADR:** #5, #6, #8; ADR-0018. **Cite:** Kairos repo; [2605.21504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21504)
  (RMSE≠alpha caution); [TimeGPT-2.1](https://www.nixtla.io/blog/timegpt-2-1-announcement).

### 4.13 Options playbook + options-aware gate  **GATED→FULL (was B-20, v0.3)**
- **Purpose:** covered-call / CSP / wheel advisory with deterministic structure selection +
  Greeks-aware caps. Equities-first discipline preserved (options ride on a validated base).
- **Spec:**
  - **Structure-selection table (deterministic, ADR-0082 A4 port):** maps (view direction,
    confidence, IV-rank, horizon, account permission) → an allowed structure set (CC / CSP /
    vertical / wheel) or **silence**. No free-text structure choice — the table decides;
    free-text never drives state (rail #6).
  - **Options-aware gate rules (ADR-0027 port):** per-position and book Greek caps (delta as
    NAV-equivalent exposure folded into the existing ladder/caps; vega and theta soft caps;
    gamma/pin-risk near expiry → forced size-down or silence); assignment-risk + expiry-proximity
    screener codes (4.2). Sizing still routes through ¼-Kelly + discrete ladder on the
    delta-equivalent.
  - **Data:** read-only broker MCP `get_option_chains/get_option_quotes/get_option_instruments`;
    asof snapshot, never streamed; abstain on stale/missing chain.
  - **No execution:** multi-leg proposals render as a structured advisory card; the human places
    the spread. The PreToolUse hook (4.8) denies any option-order tool.
- **Primitive:** `skills/options-playbook/SKILL.md` + `scripts/quantcore/options_gate.py` +
  structure table + hypothesis tests on Greek-cap invariants.
- **Rail/ADR:** #2, #3, #4, #6; ADR-0027/0028/0082.
- **Operator gate (honest):** options on an unvalidated base is rail-risk — keep behind a config
  flag and the ≥2wk equity-dogfood recommendation even though it is now fully spec'd.

### 4.14 Shadow-ledger counterfactual  **GATED→FULL (was B-22, v0.3)**
- **Purpose:** a parallel "what-if" book recording what *would* have happened without the
  human's rejections/down-sizes — measures the value (or harm) of the human override and of the
  gate's caps.
- **Spec:** `shadow.jsonl` mirrors every *proposed* (pre-HITL) action at gate-sized levels;
  settled with the same `settle.py` math on the same marks. `/retro` reports realized book vs
  shadow book (and vs a buy-and-hold + a random-control book — Vibe-Trading `run_bench_strict`
  pattern). Hash-chained + verified like the real ledger; clearly labelled non-authoritative.
- **Algorithm note:** shadow P&L is also run through alpha-after-attribution (4.5) so "the human
  cost us alpha" claims are attribution-honest, not raw-return artifacts. Informative only after
  ≥20 settled trades (state honestly).
- **Primitive:** `scripts/quantcore/shadow.py` + `/retro` integration.
- **Rail/ADR:** R10, #7; ADR-0049. **Cite:** [2508.00554](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00554) (realized-outcome reweighting).

### 4.15 Quarterly meta-retro  **GATED→FULL (was B-26, v0.4 advisory plane)**
- **Purpose:** the slow cadence of the playbook (ADR-0035) — a quarter-scale review that
  proposes (never applies) config changes: analyst weights, screener thresholds, universe,
  Kelly fraction.
- **Spec:** `/retro --quarterly` aggregates the hypothesis registry (Brier-skill trends),
  alpha-after-attribution by analyst, regime-conditioned performance, calibration drift, and
  shadow-vs-real gap. Emits an **ADVISORY** block: ranked, evidence-linked config-change
  proposals. **Nothing self-applies** — the human edits `config.json`/`gate_manifest.toml`
  (which re-digests into the ledger). Self-evolution stays advisory-plane only.
- **Anti-pattern guard:** explicitly reject ATLAS-style runtime prompt self-rewrite — prompt/
  config changes are human-applied diffs, never in-loop mutations.
- **Primitive:** `commands/retro.md` (extend) + `scripts/quantcore/meta_retro.py`.
- **Rail/ADR:** #8; ADR-0026/0035/0080/0081. **Cite:** [2510.15949](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15949) (reject auto-OPRO),
  [2602.02369](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02369) (memory-plane evolution is the safe form).

## 6. What we deliberately do NOT build (and why)

- **Lean 4 / formal theorem-proving gate** — endorsed in spirit ([2604.01483](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01483))
  but overkill for a 5-rung ladder + handful of caps; external auto-formalizer + toolchain
  breaks "standalone-installable, stdlib+pandas+pydantic, byte-identical-when-off." Stay on
  **hypothesis property tests** + an **invariant-coverage manifest** (a CI test asserting every
  gate code-path has a corresponding invariant test) — the cheap "what's proven" property

- **RL aggregator** — DO_NOT_BUILD inherited; RL training of multi-agent LLM systems is unstable
