# Hermes-Quant Deep-Work Loop Roadmap — 2026-05-27

> Generated by ARIA via deep-work-loop. Research scatter: 4 subagents on TauricResearch, HKUDS Vibe-Trading, virattt/Mai0313, SOTA papers. Codebase audit: in-line. Architecture: 4 ADRs (0039–0042). Implementation: subagent-driven across 5 waves.

## Vision

Make hermes-quant a paper-trading sidecar where:

1. **Every approval is auditable from the audit log alone** (no out-of-band probes to verify degeneracy).
2. **Every decision learns from the previous one** for the same ticker (and from cross-ticker analogs).
3. **Every analyst signal is grounded in cited data** (no LLM-fabricated price levels).
4. **The risk gate is the final authority**, supported by an adversarial 3-way risk committee that the gate consumes as evidence.
5. **Backtesting is contamination-free** — point-in-time data, knowledge-cutoff respect, walk-forward eval with transaction cost.

The reference projects (TauricResearch/TradingAgents, HKUDS/Vibe-Trading, Mai0313/TradingAgents, virattt/ai-hedge-fund) collectively encode 7 design patterns we already have, 12 we don't, and 6 named anti-patterns we should avoid. The roadmap below adopts the highest-leverage missing patterns first (P0/P1) and defers exploratory ones to v0.3+.

## Anti-patterns we explicitly do NOT adopt

- ❌ **Empty-memory hallucination** (TauricResearch fix): never let the PM prompt be "Lessons from prior decisions: " followed by an empty block. Always render `(none)` explicitly. (Wave 4 addresses.)
- ❌ **Fabricated sentiment posts** (TauricResearch v0.2.5 fix): never let the sentiment analyst skip tool calls. Forced tool-first ordering. (Wave 5.)
- ❌ **Look-ahead via LLM parametric knowledge**: any backtest covering pre-cutoff data is contaminated. Backtest harness MUST cap evaluation windows to post-cutoff. (Wave 6.)
- ❌ **Oracle Fallacy in memory**: a reflection whose outcome became knowable >= decision-asof MUST be excluded from retrieval. (Wave 4 guard test.)
- ❌ **Self-graded reflection**: the PM model that made the decision is NOT the reflector. Reflection runs on the haiku tier. (Wave 4.)
- ❌ **Single decision surface = "the LLM"**: prompt-only LLM trading is below baseline (consensus pattern C2). hermes-quant's deterministic risk gate stays the final authority; the committee is evidence, not authority. (Existing — preserved.)
- ❌ **Generative factor mining without IC dedup**: produces a Correlation Red Sea. Mandatory IC gate. (Wave 6.)
- ❌ **Confidence as accuracy proxy**: PHANTOM benchmark shows LLM confidence is uncorrelated with numerical-claim accuracy. We treat conf=1.0 as a discriminator-input, not a sizing-multiplier. (ADR-0041.)

## Success criteria per wave

**Wave 1:** `is_bma_degenerate(event)` returns False for every approval today. `state.db` reconstructs positions matching `executions.jsonl`. `quant-watchlist-evolve-daily` runs to completion in <60s. Two of {weekly, quarterly} have at least one successful real run.

**Wave 2:** Every approval since Wave 2 lands carries `target_position_pct` not None, plus stop_loss + entry_price + time_horizon. `pos=None` is impossible.

**Wave 3:** Audit log carries `risk_debate_summary` on every approval. Three risk personas debate visible in stored transcripts.

**Wave 4:** After 30 days of accumulated reflections, `HERMES_QUANT_MEMORY_INJECT=1` flip is reversible, and the A/B comparison shows ≥0 difference in outcome-quality distribution (we are not yet claiming alpha gain — just proving the system is not worse).

**Wave 5:** Verifier rejects ≥95% of analyst views with un-cited numerical claims. Drop-rate stabilizes at <5% (analysts learn the constraint).

**Wave 6:** Walk-forward backtest of any new strategy shipped post-Wave-6 is contamination-free, has a published cost model, and beats buy-and-hold on Sharpe over a 6-month rolling window before being promoted.

## Token / cost budget

- Wave 1: ~$5 in cross-model review (no LLM-runtime cost increase).
- Wave 2: ~$3 review (no runtime increase).
- Wave 3: ~$5 review + ~$0.05/decision runtime cost (3 risk personas × haiku ≈ $0.012; 2 analyst rounds ≈ $0.025; PM ≈ $0.013).
- Wave 4: ~$5 review + $0.0006/reflection (~$0.03/month at current volume).
- Wave 5: ~$3 review + ~$0.002/decision (verifier is cheap regex-and-fewshot).
- Wave 6: ~$10 review + backtest LLM cost capped via StubLLM dry-run mode.

Total roadmap LLM-runtime increase: ~$0.07/decision (vs. current ~$0.03). At 5–10 decisions/day, +$10–20/month. Acceptable.
